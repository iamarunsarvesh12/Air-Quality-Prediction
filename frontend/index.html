National Air Quality Index (NAQS) Standards

GOOD (0–50)
Minimal impact; safe for outdoor activity.
SATISFACTORY (51–100)
Minor breathing discomfort for sensitive people.
MODERATE (101–200)
Breathing discomfort to asthma patients & seniors.
POOR (201–300)
Discomfort to most on prolonged exposure.
VERY POOR (301–400)
Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure.
SEVERE (401+)
Severe health warning for entire population.
