What is AirSense?

Air pollution is linked to respiratory, cardiovascular, and long-term health problems worldwide. AirSense lets you enter local pollutant readings — PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃, along with temperature, humidity and wind speed — and returns a predicted Air Quality Index, its category, and the health effects associated with that level.

How it works

  1. 01 Enter pollutant & weather values
  2. 02 Data is validated in the browser
  3. 03 Sent to the R Plumber API
  4. 04 Random Forest model predicts AQI
  5. 05 AQI mapped to category & health effects
  6. 06 Results shown with charts & recommendations

Why air quality matters

Good (0–50)

Air quality is satisfactory; little or no risk.

Moderate (51–100)

Acceptable, but may affect very sensitive individuals.

Unhealthy (101–200)

Sensitive groups may experience health effects.

Hazardous (300+)

Serious risk of health effects for everyone.
